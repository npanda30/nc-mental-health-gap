# NC Mental Health Crisis Gap Analysis 

## Overview
Evaluating the crisis burden vs. response capacity for mental health resources in all 100 NC counties. Merged 2 county-level surveillance datasets from NC DETECT and County Health Rankings and constructed a composite index. Spatial autocorrelation (Moran's I), Ordinary Least Squares regression, and k-means clustering will indicate critical regions and contributing factors to high gap indices.

## Findings
Visualizations have thus yielded the top 15 counties by computed gap index, with Anson (3.44) and Edgecombe (3.16) as clear outliers. Additionally, the top 6 counties with highest gap index have also been shown to be rapidly increasing in suicidal ideation ED visit rates since 2020, outpacing both the state average and their own ability to support their residents.
